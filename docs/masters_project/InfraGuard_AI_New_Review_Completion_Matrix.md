## Current evidence position

The final manuscript separates automated software verification, objective
controlled-benchmark results, exploratory scoring completed by two human
operations practitioners, the distinct human System Usability Scale (SUS)
study, and security/data-governance assessment.

The practitioner exercise remains bounded because only two assessors completed
the DevOps and SRE role-lens scoring. The separate SUS study received 11
consented submissions and analysed 10 complete responses after excluding one
incomplete response without imputation. The SUS findings are descriptive and
do not establish population-level usability, reduced workload, task
performance, diagnostic correctness, or production effectiveness.
